Common questions

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open Settings → Subscription → Manage subscription, then choose Restore. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID, so signing in with the same Apple ID is enough.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Settings → Subscription → Manage subscription opens Apple’s subscription management, where you can cancel. You can also cancel from iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

I’m not receiving study reminders.

Make sure notifications are enabled in the app (Settings → Notifications) and in iOS Settings → Driver SK → Notifications.

How do I reset my progress?

Settings → Reset Progress. This permanently erases your local study data and cannot be undone.

Is my data private?

Yes. Study data stays on your device, analytics are anonymous and can be turned off in Settings → Analytics. See our Privacy Policy.
