# Privacy Policy — Driver SK

**Effective date:** 10 June 2026

Driver SK ("the app") is an offline-first study app for the Slovak driving-licence
theory test, available in Slovak, English, and Hungarian. This policy explains
what data the app handles and what your choices are.

## Summary

- The app works **without an account**. We don't know your name, email, or phone number.
- Your study progress (answers, mistakes, mock-exam results, settings) is stored
  **locally on your device** in a database. It never leaves your device.
- We collect **anonymous usage analytics** (which you can turn off in Settings).
- Purchases are processed by **Apple** and **RevenueCat**; we never see your
  payment details.

## Data stored only on your device

All study data is stored locally and is not transmitted to us:

- Your answers, mistakes, streaks, and mock-exam history
- Your language, category, notification, and readiness-mode preferences

Deleting the app deletes this data. The "Reset Progress" option in Settings also
erases it.

## Anonymous analytics (PostHog)

To understand how the app is used and improve it, we collect anonymous usage
events through PostHog (PostHog EU/US, posthog.com), such as:

- Screens viewed and buttons tapped
- Study activity (e.g. a question was answered correctly/incorrectly), mock-exam
  outcomes, onboarding progress
- App errors and crashes
- Device type, OS version, app version, and a **random installation identifier**

This identifier is random, created on first launch, and is not linked to your
identity. We do not collect names, email addresses, contacts, location, photos,
or advertising identifiers, and we do not track you across other apps or
websites.

**Opt-out:** Settings → Analytics. When opted out, no analytics events are sent.

## Purchases (Apple & RevenueCat)

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat
(revenuecat.com) to validate purchases and manage subscription status.
RevenueCat receives an anonymous app-user identifier and your purchase receipt
from Apple — never your payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at
https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

## Notifications

Study reminders are **local notifications**, scheduled on your device. No push
tokens are created and no notification data is sent to any server. You can
disable each reminder in Settings or in the system notification settings.

## Data sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties beyond the service
providers named above (PostHog for analytics, RevenueCat/Apple for purchases),
who process it on our behalf.

## Children

The app is intended for users preparing for a driving licence (15+). It
collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Your rights (GDPR)

Analytics data is anonymous and cannot be linked back to you. If you have any
question or request regarding your data, contact us and we will help, including
deletion of analytics data associated with your installation identifier where
technically possible.

## Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The current version is always
