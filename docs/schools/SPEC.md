# Driver for driving schools: spec (MVP)

Online accounts and a small CRM for Slovak driving schools (autoškoly), sold directly to schools,
with the Driver SK app as the student's side. Code: `schools/` (Rust API + web dashboard) and the
flagged "Autoškola" feature in the app. Status and how to run: `docs/schools/REPORT.md`.

## 1. What a Slovak autoškola must keep and produce (and what we do about it)

Sources read for this: zákon č. 93/2005 Z. z. o autoškolách (znenie účinné 1. 9. 2026,
slov-lex PDF), vyhláška č. 45/2016 Z. z. (konsolidované znenie 23. 5. 2023, PDF mindop.sk),
vyhláška č. 9/2009 Z. z. prílohy č. 2 a 2b (PDF forms in circulation), the JISCD operator pages and
the JISCD curriculum table for group B. Paragraph numbers below are from those texts. I read the two primary PDFs myself; the web summaries disagreed with each other on annex numbers, so trust the PDFs.

The decisive fact. Since 2016 the mandatory records are kept in JISCD (Jednotný
informačný systém v cestnej doprave, "cestný informačný systém"), not on paper and not in the
school's own software:

| Obligation | Where it says so | What it means for us |
|---|---|---|
| Documentation of every course and of the technical base is kept electronically in the information system | z. 93/2005 § 6 ods. 1 písm. j) | We **cannot replace JISCD**. We complement it. |
| Attendance at theory, simulator and driving is recorded by approved identification devices (tokens in the car, classroom, simulator) that send data to JISCD | § 6 ods. 1 písm. k), § 10a ods. 1 písm. i)–m), vyhl. § 7 ods. 4, prílohy 4 a 5 | Our attendance and driving records are the school's own working copy, never the legal record. |
| The course documents are: elektronická matričná kniha, elektronická triedna kniha, elektronický výkaz vozidla, trenažérová kniha, autocvičisková kniha, all in JISCD | vyhl. § 16–21 | Our student record mirrors the **matričná kniha fields** (§ 17 ods. 2) so a school can keep both consistent. |
| The osvedčenie o absolvovaní kurzu is issued through JISCD | vyhl. § 8 ods. 19; contents § 23 | We generate a *Potvrdenie* with the same § 23 fields, labelled as the school's confirmation, not the osvedčenie. |
| Medical (and, where required, psychological) fitness is recorded in JISCD before enrolment | § 6 ods. 1 písm. h), i) | We track the dates as a checklist so the admin sees who is missing what. |
| Every student gets JISCD access to see their own recorded training, if they give an e-mail | § 6 ods. 1 písm. p) | Not ours. |
| Before enrolment the applicant provides: meno, dátum a miesto narodenia, rodné číslo, adresa pobytu, e-mail a telefón, súhlas zákonného zástupcu ak < 18, doklady pre písm. h) a i) | § 2b ods. 3 | Exactly our student fields. Group B also needs proof of nižšie stredné vzdelanie (§ 2b ods. 2); earliest enrolment 12 months before the licence age (§ 2b ods. 1). |
| Start, duration, structure, content and price of the course are agreed before it starts | vyhl. § 7 ods. 3 | Our **training agreement** PDF. |
| Lesson = 45 min; max 9 lessons a day per student; theory max 6 h a day in one block; driving max 2 h AT/AC and 3 h CP a day; theory groups max 30; course max 12 months; at least 50 % of theory in person, rest in JISCD e-learning | vyhl. § 7 ods. 2, § 8 ods. 1, 2, 3, 6, 11 | Encoded as validation and shown as hints. |
| Group B curriculum (JISCD table, "s AC a AT, bez NVP"): theory 32 h (PCP 20, NVÚ 2, TVV 3, ZBJ 4, OP 3); driving 39 h (AT 4, AC 2 + 2, flexi 6, CP min 24, PÚ 1); ≥ 15 km per driving lesson on average | JISCD učebné osnovy príloha 2 | Defaults for a new B course (32 / 39), A: 26 / 18. |
| The school applies for the exam with *Prihláška na skúšku z odbornej spôsobilosti* (list of applicants with rodné číslo and address) plus each applicant's *Žiadosť o udelenie vodičského oprávnenia* in two copies and the documents of § 15 vyhl. 9/2009 | z. 8/2009 § 80 ods. 2; vyhl. 9/2009 prílohy 2 a 2b | **Prihláška** is generated by us with the form's text. The Žiadosť is a strict official A4 form filled by the applicant and a doctor; we do not reproduce it (unsure whether a software-filled copy is accepted; left for later). |
| Fines: § 6 písm. j) breaches 1 000–5 000 €; písm. e), k), r) 5 000–10 000 € | z. 93/2005 § 17 ods. 1 | Why the "JISCD stays the source of truth" line matters in sales conversations. |
| Course certificate only after proof of a first-aid course and exam | § 6 ods. 3 | Checklist item; the certificate text names it. |

Unsure / to verify with a school or lawyer: retention periods for the school's own copies (the old
vyhláška 349/2005 said 3 years after the course; the current one delegates to JISCD); whether
authorities accept a software-printed Žiadosť; the exact wording schools use in their zmluva.

What we can generate (built): Prihláška na skúšku (group), Potvrdenie o absolvovaní kurzu
(student), Zmluva o vykonaní vodičského kurzu (student), Súhlas zákonného zástupcu + GDPR notice
(student), Výpis z prípravy (student, from app data), Prezenčná listina teórie (group).

## 2. Roles

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| **School admin** (prevádzkovateľ / zodpovedný zástupca) | everything in the school: profile, courses, students, lessons, documents, codes |
| **Instructor** | the same minus school profile edits (MVP: no per-course restriction; later: only own courses) |
| **Student** | the app: join a school with a code, see what is shared, sync, leave. No web login. |

One login belongs to one school. Parents have no login; they get the Výpis PDF.

## 3. Data model (Postgres, `schools/server/migrations`)

`schools` (legal identity for documents) → `branches`; `users` (admin / instructor, password) with
`sessions`; `groups` = courses (category, JISCD course no., dates, exam date, lead instructor,
required theory/driving lessons, status planned/running/finished/archived); `students` (§ 2b
fields, guardian, document checklist dates, GDPR dates, register no. = poradové číslo); `enrolments`
(student × group: status, price, paid, finished, certificate issued); `join_codes` (group code or
one-use student code, expiry); `app_links` (device, hashed bearer token, lang, last sync, revoked);
`theory_lessons` + `theory_attendance`; `driving_lessons` (AT/AC/CP/PU, lessons, km, vehicle,
notes); synced from the app: `app_attempts` (one row per answer, the app's own ids), `app_mock_exams`,
`app_exam_results`, `app_snapshots` (readiness numbers the app computed, last 30 per student);
`documents` (kind, payload, PDF bytes).

Readiness on the server is computed with the app's formula (`readiness.rs`, a port of
`src/lib/readiness.js`): mistakes 30 / 7-day accuracy 25 / mocks 30 / coverage 15, ready ≥ 97.
Seen count, accuracy and mock history come from the synced events; open-mistake count, bank size,
the forecast and the streak come from the latest snapshot (the server cannot derive them).

## 4. Screens (web, Slovak UI with an English switch)

- **Prehľad**: active and linked students, average readiness, ready/almost counts, who studied
  this week, readiness distribution, upcoming exams, real-exam pass rate, running courses.
- **Kurzy**: table with per-course readiness, attendance, driving average, exam date; new course.
- **Kurz**: stats tiles; students ranked by readiness with questions seen, mistakes, mocks,
  last activity, theory %, driving h; join code card; theory lessons with attendance editing;
  documents (Prihláška, Prezenčná listina).
- **Žiaci**: search, filter by course, readiness, last activity; new student.
- **Žiak**: readiness card (components, trend sparkline, weak categories, mocks, real exam),
  driving lessons (+ add), theory, app link card with personal code, documents (generate + list),
  editable personal data and the legal checklist, enrolments.
- **Autoškola**: legal data printed on documents, people, branches.

## 5. How the app links to a school

1. The school makes a code: a **group code** (valid 30 days, any number of uses) on the course
   page, or a **personal code** (one use, 14 days) on a student's card.
2. In the app, Settings → Autoškola → the student types the code. `POST /api/v1/app/join` with
   `{code, device_id, lang, app_version, first_name?, last_name?}`. A personal code links that
   student; a group code creates the student from the typed name (422 `name_required` first), or
   finds the same device's earlier student in that group (reinstall).
3. The answer carries a bearer token (SHA-256 kept server-side) and what to show: school, course,
   student name. The app stores them on its settings row and resets `synced_at` marks so the
   whole history goes up at the first sync.
4. Sync (`POST /api/v1/app/sync`) runs on Home focus at most every 10 minutes, and on demand:
   unsynced `answer_attempts`, completed `mock_exams`, `exam_results`, plus a snapshot. The
   server upserts by the app's ids, so retries are safe; rows are marked only after a 200.
5. Leave (`POST /api/v1/app/unlink`) revokes the token; a 401 later (school revoked) drops the
   local link. QR codes: not built (a code is 8 readable characters); the join URL scheme
   `driver://school?code=…` is the obvious next step.

The app stays fully offline and unchanged when the flag is off or nothing is linked.

## 6. Privacy and GDPR

- **Controller** is the school; we are its processor (a DPA is needed before the first paying
  school). Legal bases for the school's processing: contract (čl. 6 ods. 1 písm. b) and legal
  obligation (písm. c: z. 93/2005 § 2b, § 6; z. 8/2009 § 77–80). Sharing app progress is a
  separate, voluntary **consent** (písm. a), given by joining in the app and revocable by leaving;
  the generated GDPR notice says so, and the app says what is and is not sent.
- **Minors**: students are often 16–17. The law already requires the guardian's consent for
  enrolment (§ 2b ods. 3 písm. g); our guardian form covers enrolment and the app sharing. The
  app itself needs no account and no age gate: the code links an existing training relationship.
- **Rodné číslo** is stored only because the exam application needs it; it is shown only on the
  student card and in that PDF. Retention proposal: course + 5 years (accounting 10 years) with a
  purge job; not implemented in MVP. Deleting a student cascades to everything synced.
- Transport: HTTPS in production (cookie `Secure`, SameSite=Lax, CSRF by Origin check); tokens
  are random 256-bit and hashed at rest; passwords argon2.

## 7. Pricing assumptions (to validate)

Slovakia has roughly 480 driving schools (our October 2026 scrape of zlatestranky, deduplicated),
many with 1–3 branches; B courses cost 850–1 300 €. Working assumption: **29 €/month per school
up to 60 active students, 49 €/month above**, billed yearly with two months free; the student's
app is free when linked to a paying school (the school pays instead of the 15 % store cut). That
puts a 30-school pilot at about 1 000–1 500 €/month. Competitors seen: Anolla (booking of
driving lessons), Czech "Moje autoškola"; nothing Slovak that combines JISCD-aware records with
the student's own theory progress.

## 8. MVP cut versus later

**Built (this branch):** API with auth, courses, students, enrolments, join codes, theory and
driving records, app sync and readiness, six PDF documents, web dashboard, app flag + screen +
sync, demo seed, tests (Rust unit + integration, web vitest, app Jest), one-command dev stack.

**Later, in rough order of value:**
1. Instructor invites, password reset, per-instructor course scoping.
2. QR / deep-link join; push the school's exam date into the app's exam-date setting.
3. Driving-lesson booking (the thing Anolla sells) and instructor calendars.
4. Payments and invoices per enrolment; export for the accountant.
5. JISCD alignment: import the JISCD course number and participant list (no public API known;
   probably CSV); mark lessons "recorded in JISCD".
6. Student web view / parent view of the Výpis.
7. Hungarian dashboard (schools in the south). English is done (SK/EN switch, 17 Oct 2026); Hungarian is one more catalog in `web/src/i18n`.
8. Hosting: one small VM or Fly machine, Postgres, backups; the binary already serves the web
   build; e-mail provider for invites.
